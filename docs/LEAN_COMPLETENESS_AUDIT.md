## Source and statement audit

The definition `MohP3.arithRank I : ℕ∞` is the infimum of the cardinalities
of finite sets `s` satisfying
`(Ideal.span (s : Set A)).radical = I.radical`. This is the standard arithmetic
rank, with infinity when no finite set works. It does not encode the desired
answer in its definition. `Ideal.height` is Mathlib's Krull height.

The polynomial and formal-power-series ideals, their four generators, the
two degree-16 witnesses, and the maps with images
`t^6+t^31`, `t^8`, `t^10` retain the definitions from the preceding audit.
All thirteen preceding library modules are byte-for-byte unchanged except
`FormalLocal.lean`, whose only change is an explanatory comment. No proof or
definition in those modules changed.

The six new height/arithmetic-rank theorem statements are:

- `MohP3.height_P`: `P.height = 2` over `ℚ`;
- `MohP3.arithRank_P`: `arithRank P = 2` over `ℚ`;
- `MohP3.height_Ppoly`: `(Ppoly k).height = 2`;
- `MohP3.arithRank_Ppoly`: `arithRank (Ppoly k) = 2`;
- `MohP3.FormalLocal.height_Pk`: `(Pk k).height = 2`;
- `MohP3.FormalLocal.arithRank_Pk`: `arithRank (Pk k) = 2`.

The field-parametrized results require only `k : Type*`, `[Field k]`, and
`[CharZero k]`. The kernel identifications and unconditional radical
equalities from the earlier development are retained.

The lower height bound uses a strict prime chain `⊥ < Psurf < P` (and its
field and power-series versions). Its strictness is proved using the nonzero
element `y^5-z^4` and the failure of `f4` to vanish under the intermediate
surface parametrization. Factorization of the Moh map proves containment.

The upper bound uses Mathlib's Krull height theorem for a minimal prime over
the ideal generated by the two witnesses. The arithmetic-rank lower bound
applies the same theorem to every finite set of equations with the required
radical, then takes the infimum.

For power series, `MvPowerSeriesAux.lean` constructs ring isomorphisms for
reindexing and splitting off a variable. It derives Noetherianity by induction
from Mathlib's univariate power-series theorem. Noetherianity is proved here,
not assumed as an additional hypothesis of the final theorem.

The project Lean sources have no occurrences of `sorry`, `admit`, added
`axiom`, `constant`, `native_decide`, `unsafe`, `implemented_by`, or kernel-check
bypass options. The declarative Lake configuration has no custom build hook.
The auxiliary Python scripts are not invoked by the Lean build.
